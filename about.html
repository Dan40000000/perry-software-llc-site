Software Grounded in Practice Operations

Nuvora Health brings clinical, administrative, and revenue workflows together with guided implementation and direct service.

Our Approach

Perry Software LLC develops Nuvora Health for the day-to-day work of medical practices. The platform is organized around the handoffs that matter: scheduling and intake, clinical documentation, results and follow-up, charge review, billing work, patient communication, and operational reporting.

Dermatology is a central focus. That means the product and implementation conversations account for specialty workflows such as body diagrams, lesion and photography records, biopsy and pathology tracking, procedures, recalls, and the connection between clinical work and revenue activity.

Based in Lehi, Utah, Perry Software LLC works directly with practices to understand requirements, define the implementation scope, configure selected workflows, prepare users, and support the transition to Nuvora Health.

How We Work With Practices

Software selection should be specific. We begin by reviewing the practice structure, current systems, essential workflows, user roles, locations, data requirements, external connections, and operational priorities. The resulting quote and implementation scope document what is included.

Demonstrations are tailored to the workflows a practice wants to evaluate. That gives providers, managers, front-desk teams, clinical staff, and billers a practical way to assess how Nuvora Health would fit their work.

Guided Transition and Direct Service

Changing systems requires coordinated decisions. The transition plan identifies the source data, field mappings, validation steps, configuration, training, responsibilities, timing, and go-live support appropriate to the agreed scope.

After implementation, support terms and contact paths remain documented so the practice knows where to bring product questions, workflow needs, and account requests.

Simplicity

Software should make work easier, not harder. We obsess over usability.

Transparency

Quotes identify selected modules, user and location rates, implementation work, external costs, and contract terms.

Partnership

Implementation and support begin with clear owners, practical checkpoints, and direct communication.
