The Transition Framework

A Practical Path From Review to Go-Live

Every transition is shaped by the source system, available exports, record volume, connected services, and practice workflow. These phases keep the work visible.

PH1

Phase One

Discovery and Source Review

  • Discovery session — review your current system, workflows, priorities, and operational constraints
  • Source assessment — identify available exports, record types, file formats, and access requirements
  • Written scope — document included data, exclusions, responsibilities, checkpoints, and proposed timing
  • Practice participation — identify the people who will answer source-data and workflow questions
PH2

Phase Two

Mapping and Configuration

  • Field mapping — align agreed source fields with their destination in Nuvora Health
  • Data preparation — normalize and prepare records according to the approved mapping
  • Workflow configuration — configure templates, users, roles, locations, and selected workflows
  • Connection planning — define required external connections, owners, dependencies, and testing steps
PH3

Phase Three

Validation and Team Readiness

  • Validation samples — review representative records, counts, and critical fields with your designated team members
  • Workflow testing — walk through scheduling, documentation, billing, and follow-up scenarios in scope
  • Role-based training — prepare providers, front desk, clinical staff, billers, and administrators for their work
  • Readiness review — document open items, owners, and the agreed go-live approach
PH4

Phase Four

Cutover and Supported Go-Live

  • Final preparation: complete the agreed cutover steps, validation checks, and approval sign-offs
  • Source cutoff: follow the written plan for final entries, exports, and read-only access
  • Cutover: complete the final scoped import and confirm the agreed validation checklist
  • Go-live support: route questions and issues directly to the implementation team during the defined support window
