OAK EMR is based in Lehi, Utah. This page is a planning resource for Utah practices; it does not imply a separate local office or an existing customer list across the state.

A statewide workflow lens

Whether your team is evaluating a new platform or planning a change, start with the work that crosses roles. A useful demonstration follows the practice day and makes assumptions visible before a quote is prepared.

Connect the workday

Review appointment types, intake, messages, documentation, follow-up, and checkout as one connected sequence.

Give each role a clear view

Map provider, front-desk, clinical, and billing responsibilities to the screens and handoffs each role uses.

Measure the full scope

Separate recurring platform rates from implementation, migration, connections, usage-based services, and support terms.

Scope the work around your practice

Start with roles, locations, and workflows

List the providers, staff users, locations, templates, patient communications, reports, and external connections that matter. This gives the team a concrete agenda for a walkthrough and a written scope.

Keep safeguards in the conversation

Review role-based access, audit logging, encryption, customer responsibilities, and the details that apply to your proposed environment. Read the OAK EMR security overview.

Use a written migration plan

A responsible transition accounts for source-system access, data mapping, cleaning, configuration, validation, training, and cutover ownership. OAK EMR can document those decisions in an implementation scope before final pricing is approved.
