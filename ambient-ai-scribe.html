The Visit-to-Review Workflow

Documentation that keeps moving after the conversation ends.

The scribe assists with the administrative work around an encounter. It does not replace clinical judgment or billing review.

01

Confirm, then capture

After the clinician confirms the appropriate patient-consent process, the appointment audio is captured and linked to the visit.

02

Transcribe the conversation

The appointment becomes a working transcript so the clinician can focus on the patient instead of documenting every sentence in real time.

03

Build the clinical draft

OAK EMR organizes SOAP content, a visit summary, differential diagnoses, suggested tests, follow-up tasks, and code suggestions.

04

Review and carry forward

The clinician edits and approves the content before applying it to the encounter. Supported suggestions can then enter the billing-review workflow.

Documentation to Billing Review

Keep the clinical story and the revenue workflow connected.

When the clinician applies an approved assistant response, OAK EMR can prepare diagnosis information, pending charge lines, and a billing-review item. That reduces re-entry while preserving the review steps needed before anything reaches a claim.

  • Suggestions remain connected to the source encounter
  • Billing reviewers see what requires confirmation
  • Missing documentation can be surfaced before release
  • Claims are never released solely because the AI suggested a code

Straight Answers

What practices usually ask.

Does the AI scribe replace the clinician?

No. It prepares a draft and suggestions. The clinician remains responsible for reviewing the documentation and making the final clinical decisions.

Does it automatically submit claims?

No. It can prepare suggested codes, pending charge lines, and billing-review tasks. A clinician or billing professional must confirm the final codes, diagnoses, units, modifiers, and claim readiness.

What does it create from the appointment?

A working transcript, structured SOAP draft, visit summary, and supported suggestions for diagnoses, tests, follow-up work, and coding review.
