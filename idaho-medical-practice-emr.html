OAK EMR is based in Lehi, Utah. This page is a planning resource for practices in Idaho; it does not imply an Idaho office or existing local customers.

Build around the work your team shares

A useful evaluation follows the handoffs between scheduling, clinical work, patient communication, billing review, and support. Use the walkthrough to test the paths that create friction today and identify the responsibilities that need documentation.

Lean team handoffs

Keep appointment status, messages, intake, checkout, and follow-up visible to the roles that move the day forward.

Specialty documentation

Review templates, voice dictation, reusable macros, and the clinician approval steps for the records your practice creates.

Billing review

Separate supported suggestions from final decisions so billing staff can confirm codes, units, modifiers, and claim readiness.

Document what changes at cutover

Source data and validation

Identify the records, exports, templates, attachments, and status history that need review. The implementation plan should name mapping decisions, validation checkpoints, and any data that remains outside the agreed scope.

Training and support

Role-based training and a defined support path help providers and staff prepare for the workflows they will use. OAK EMR describes support coverage and escalation paths in the implementation plan.

  • Which roles, locations, workflows, and connections must be ready for go-live?
  • Who owns source exports, review decisions, training attendance, and final validation?
  • Which security requirements and customer responsibilities should be addressed during review?

Review safeguards early

Role-based access, audit logging, encrypted connections, vendor responsibilities, and customer controls all deserve specific questions. Read the OAK EMR security overview and bring your requirements to the conversation.

Compare the full planning scope

Public per-provider planning rates are only a starting point. A matched-scope written quote governs final pricing and separates recurring software from migration, implementation, connections, usage-based services, and support terms. Review the pricing approach.

Review OAK EMR with your team

A short demo can make the next decision concrete without assuming a universal Idaho configuration or timeline. Bring the workflow and migration questions specific to your practice.
