OAK EMR is based in Lehi, Utah. This page is a planning resource for practices in St. George; it does not imply a local office or existing local customers.

Start with dermatology's real handoffs

Choose a platform by following the work from the front desk to the clinical record and then into follow-up and revenue review. A short walkthrough can use your actual roles and handoffs instead of a generic feature list.

Scheduling and intake

Review appointment types, reminders, intake status, check-in, checkout, and same-day changes in one conversation.

Photos and pathology

Map lesion photos, specimen details, results, patient notification, and follow-up tasks to the record workflow.

Billing review

Trace charge capture, supported code suggestions, claim readiness, denials, payments, and open work for the billing team.

Ask focused questions before you switch

  • Which providers, staff roles, locations, templates, photos, and historical records are in scope?
  • How will pathology follow-up, recalls, patient messages, and incomplete tasks be reviewed during validation?
  • Which connections, payment services, communications, and support responsibilities need written confirmation?
  • How will clinicians review documentation drafts and how will billing staff confirm final charges?

Make migration scope visible

The source system, data condition, workflow decisions, training plan, validation checkpoints, and cutover responsibilities should be discussed before a final quote. OAK EMR describes the migration work and support coverage in the proposed implementation scope.

Bring the source-system details

A redacted inventory of record types, exports, interfaces, templates, and timing gives the team a useful starting point. The resulting plan can separate what is included from what still needs a decision.

Review the workflow in a short demo

Use a 15-minute conversation to show the roles and dermatology handoffs that matter to your practice. We can then identify the questions needed for a matched-scope quote and migration plan.
