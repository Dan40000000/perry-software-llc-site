OAK EMR is based in Lehi, Utah. This page is a planning resource for practices in Las Vegas and Southern Nevada; it does not imply a Nevada office or existing local customers.

Design for busy multi-role days

Start with the handoffs your team must coordinate: appointments, intake, clinical documentation, messages, follow-up, charges, claims, and payments. A focused demo helps each role evaluate the workflow it actually owns.

Front-desk throughput

Review scheduling, reminders, intake status, check-in, balances, checkout, and same-day rebooking.

Provider review

Follow templates, voice dictation, ambient drafts, edits, approvals, orders, and follow-up from one record workflow.

Revenue queues

Make charge capture, claim review, denials, payments, and account work visible to the billing team.

Compare recurring and one-time scope

Ask about connections and usage

List the interfaces, communications, payment services, locations, users, and usage-based services that could change a total. Review the public planning rates and the matched-scope quote approach.

Keep the operating assumptions explicit

Clarify which modules, support responsibilities, training activities, migration tasks, and validation steps are included before approval. A written quote should separate recurring software from one-time work.

  • Which roles and locations need a shared view of work during a busy day?
  • Which source records, templates, attachments, and status history should be reviewed for migration?
  • Which access, audit, encryption, vendor, and customer-responsibility questions belong in security review?

Plan a controlled transition

Migration preparation can include source review, data mapping, cleaning, workflow configuration, training, validation, and a defined go-live support path. Review the migration process and bring the decisions specific to your practice.

Review safeguards with your team

OAK EMR describes role-based access, auditability, encrypted connections, operational safeguards, and customer responsibilities for review. Read the security overview before you assemble your requirements.
